Why your browser may show a warning

Tor onion connections are already end-to-end encrypted and authenticated by the onion address. Torkitten adds HTTPS as another encrypted layer for browser compatibility, secure cookies, login, and two-factor authentication.

Torkitten uses its own private certificate authority so your onion address does not have to be submitted to a public certificate authority or published through Certificate Transparency. Your browser does not initially recognize this private authority, so it may show a warning.

Install the public certificate

This only teaches this device to trust certificates from your Torkitten. It contains no private key and does not add another encryption layer.

Public root certificate

iPhone or iPad: approve the downloaded profile in Settings and, if required, Certificate Trust Settings. Android or computer: import the public root as a CA certificate into the browser or system trust store you intend to use.

Public certificate alternative

The Tor Project currently lists DigiCert EV and HARICA DV for publicly trusted onion certificates. This may disclose the onion hostname to the authority and through Certificate Transparency.
